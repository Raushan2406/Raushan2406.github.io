📧 QA Weekly Performance Report · Automation

Google Apps Script time trigger: every Monday 09:00 IST → runWeeklyReport()

What one run does:

  1. Recalculates each audit's fiscal week from the roster (week-offs and night shifts).
  2. Builds each QA's weekly report: daily breakdown, audit types, target achievement, disputes, overtime.
  3. Picks the top performer per LOB and sends every QA their email (CC leads).
  4. Sends the manager the Red / Yellow / Green team report.
