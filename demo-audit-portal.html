Quality Assurance Operating Guidelines, Dropdown Dictionary & Portal Usage Adherence
Scroll to bottom to unlock acknowledgment
Mandatory Auditor Requirement: Every QA Auditor must read this operating procedure completely. Every dropdown parameter, rule, and interface feature is detailed below. The checkbox and acceptance button will remain permanently locked until you scroll through to the end of this manual.
1. Portal Usages & Anti-Bot Adherence Guidelines
  • Sequential Matrix Lock (Q1 to Q19): Evaluation questions must be assessed sequentially. Question N+1 remains strictly disabled until Question N is manually evaluated. Programmatic keyboard navigation, injection scripts, and auto-click browser extensions are actively intercepted and blocked.
  • Zero-Tolerance Fatal Error Rule (Question 1): Question 1 evaluates "Was the case handled correctly?". Marking No automatically reduces the total audit score to 0%, regardless of the choices in questions 2 through 19.
  • Strict Coaching Note Enforcement: If any parameter from Question 1 through Question 19 is marked "No", entering a detailed, actionable coaching note is mandatory. Filling in N/A, NA, None, -, or notes shorter than 15 characters will trigger an adherence violation alert and block audit submission.
  • Binary Scoring Enforcement: Questions 8 through 19 strictly accept only Yes or No. NA is permitted only on questions 2 through 7 where applicable.
  • Cursor Click Mandatory on Custom Dropdowns: All dropdown triggers use guard containers. Options must be chosen with explicit mouse clicks. Direct shortcut-driven selections are blocked.
  • Agent Name Integrity Check: In queues requiring manual associate tagging (Reopen, FCR, General Audit), names must match the Salesforce master roster. Inputting timestamps, dates, or non-roster entries triggers a validation error and halts audit submission.
  • Timer Audit Trail: The portal measures audit start time and completion time. Submissions completed unnaturally fast (< 60 seconds) without comprehensive case review represent an adherence violation.
2. Interface & Dashboard Controls
  • Theme Selector (Classic / Pro): Toggles between clean daylight styling and high-contrast dark mode. Selection persists in session storage.
  • QA Guidelines Button: Re-opens this reference manual at any time from the top navigation bar.
  • Left Panel (QA Check Reminders): Displays active operational quality watchouts and calibration reminders synced from the Config sheet.
  • Right Panel (QA Notice Board): Displays live policy updates, calibration announcements, and direct documentation hyperlinks.
  • Audit Code: The target audit queue to evaluate (e.g., Reopen, FCR, CSAT, SLA_First_Response, SLA_First_Resolve, SLA_Final_Resolve, Need Info Abandoned, ADR, AHT, General Audit, BPO Coaching).
  • Fetch Next: Fetches the oldest un-audited case for the selected queue while automatically excluding previously audited or skipped cases.
  • Skip Button: Marks a case in the SkippedCases tab under your email ID if self-handled or corrupted, and retrieves the next record.
  • Touch Counter: Numeric integer recording the total number of agent responses/touches made on the ticket.
  • Case Accept Date & Time: Timestamp entered in DD/MM/YYYY HH:MM format noting when the associate accepted ticket ownership.

4. Complete Dropdown Dictionary & Selection Criteria

A. Reopen Queue Dropdowns

  • Root Cause Type → Subcategory:
    • System:
      • System issue: Software glitches, automated email formatting breakdowns, or platform errors.
      • Other - see remark: Unlisted system failures; strictly restricted to maximum 7 words.
    • Process\Policy:
      • Duplicate Case Handling Process: Ticket reopened following duplicate ticket handling or merging.
      • HR Template Used: Mandatory Search Selection. Use the live search bar inside the HR Template dropdown to search and select the exact macro template name sent by the agent.
      • UA Template Used: Agent utilized an unapproved template. Strictly restricted to maximum 7 words.
      • IRIS-GWF/Agentic AI Followed: Agent followed approved AI workflows correctly, but the seller reopened. Pre-populates the required inquiry prompt as background placeholder text.
      • Other - see remark: Procedural gap not listed; strictly restricted to maximum 7 words.
    • Seller:
      • Seller ask new question: Seller replied with an unrelated new question.
      • Seller wants to keep case open for verification: Seller requests delay to verify inventory or internal records.
      • Additional clarification requested by seller: Clarification sought on provided solution without disputing the outcome.
      • Dissatisfied_with_Solution: Unlocks 3rd Level Detail Dropdown:
        • DIM Dispute - Not Accept the Reimbursement Value: Contests reimbursement payout for dimension adjustments.
        • DIM - Request to verify past Historical fees: Demands audit of historical monthly fees.
        • DIM Dispute - Need Remeasured Photos from FC: Demands Fulfillment Center photographic measurement proof.
        • DIM Dispute - Not Accept the Remeasured Data: Disagrees with physical remeasurement figures.
        • IBQ Dispute - Not Accept the Reimbursement Value: Contests Inbound Quality dispute compensation.
        • IBQ Dispute - Not accepting the Overage receives: Seller disputes warehouse overage counts.
        • IBQ Dispute - 10 days dispute window: Reopened due to 10-day eligibility rules.
        • ITS Shipment - Within 10 Days: Carrier transit dispute raised inside 10-day window.
        • IBQ Dispute - 50 days dispute window / Refund Dispute - 45 days dispute window: Disagreements over dispute window boundaries.
        • Not Satisfied with Reimbursement Amount: General dispute of calculated credit amount.
        • Refund Dispute -others (details see remark) / Other - see remark: Custom dispute scenario (strictly max 7 words).
      • Dissatisfied_with_Process_Policy: Unlocks 3rd Level Detail Dropdown:
        • Buy Box Loss: Reopened protesting Buy Box suppression.
        • Negative Reviews - Not meeting criteria: Review removal requests that fail policy criteria.
        • Negative Reviews - logistics/ shipping issues: Review removal requests tied to courier delays.
        • Restricted sale for international sellers: Contesting cross-border seller limitations.
        • Sellers can't cancel the WFS Order: Demanding manual order cancellation outside policy.
        • Sellers can't change the product ID for WFS Item: Contesting product ID lockdown rules.
        • Duplicate variants found despite seller claim: Policy enforcement disputes.
      • Dissactified_with_being_referred_to_3rd_party: Unlocks 3rd Level Detail Dropdown:
        • Refered to Carrier For shipment missing: Unhappy with courier redirection.
        • Refered to CCA for refund/cancel/late order: Unhappy with Customer Care redirection.
        • Refered to Account Manager - Event: Protesting account management escalation rules.
    • Agent:
      • Agent click wrong case status: Associate prematurely closed or set incorrect ticket status.
      • Incompleted solution(Multiple questions in a case): Associate addressed only some of multiple questions raised.
      • Incompleted solution(1 question in a case): Incomplete, vague, or partial resolution provided.
      • Incorrect Resolution: Associate provided incorrect instructions or wrong policy guidance.
    • AutoReply_ThankYou_Blank:
      • Thank you / Email auto reply from seller / Blank mail from seller.

B. FCR (First Contact Resolution) Queue Dropdowns

  • FCR Category: Choose between System, Process/Policy, Seller, Agent, AutoReply_ThankYou_Blank, Reimbursement, or Other.
  • Alternative Sub Reason (Dynamic):
    • If Additional Information Required from Seller is chosen, select the exact artifact requested:
      • Requested Documentation (UPC Doc / Brand Ownership Claim / Other)
      • Requested error screenshot / elaboration / additional details
      • Requested Feed ID / Item ID
      • Requested Disputes Template / POD / Packing List / BOL
      • Requested POD / RSL / Clear image of Return Item / Attachments
      • Requested Tracking ID / Order ID
      • DIMS - Physical images of item measurement/Seller's expected dimensions
    • If Cross Question - Related to Same Issue is chosen, select the seller's follow-up:
      • Not satisfied with reimbursement, Provided Additional Item ID, Provided Error Feed ID, Provided Shipment ID / Order ID / Tracking ID, Provided Additional Removal Order, or Asked for additional clarification.
    • If Escalated to External Team is chosen, select the target partner queue:
      • Escalated to Fixit, Escalated to TechOps JIRA, Escalated to Production JIRA, Escalated to Canada Service Now, Escalated to ITS Request, Escalated to SSS Opt Out, Escalated to Strikethrough, Escalated to Slack, Escalated to Outlook, or Escalated to Team.
    • If Incorrect/Partial Resolution Provided is chosen:
      • Select Acuitty Agent Fault or Non-Acuitty Agent Fault.

C. CSAT Queue Dropdowns

  • Error Type:
    • Agent: The associate caused the seller dissatisfaction through poor communication, incorrect advice, or handling delays. Selecting "Agent" automatically forces CSAT Audit Status to "Valid" and mandates a comprehensive Coaching Note.
    • Policy: Dissatisfaction was solely caused by platform rules or limitations (fee schedule, dispute deadlines).
    • System: Dissatisfaction was driven by technical bugs or platform errors.
  • CSAT Audit Status: Choose Valid (defect upheld against agent/operation) or Invalid (agent handled case flawlessly according to SOP).

D. SLA Dropdowns (SLA_First_Response, SLA_First_Resolve, SLA_Final_Resolve)

  • Reason Selection: Choose the precise breach cause (e.g., Agent Error - Agent Delay in Response, Kept in Verify - SOP Followed, Received after SLA Breach - Handled by another Team, Escalated to External Team/ Created JIRA, Case assigned after 48 Hrs SLA).
  • Agent Fault: Must be marked Yes if the assigned associate caused the breach through inaction or failing to follow verify-status timelines, or No if breach occurred prior to queue intake or due to external bottlenecks.

E. ADR & AHT Queue Dropdowns

  • Reason: Choose between:
    • Agent error - Delay in Response / Agent error - Unnecessary Handling
    • Agent error - Incorrect Handling
    • Escalated to External Team (Unlocks Sub-Reason: Canada Service now, IRIS, ITS Request, Outlook, Pricing Anamoly, Production JIRA, Review Refresh, SFS Team, SSS Optout, Strikethrough, Supervisor Escalation, Techops JIRA, UGC)
    • Handled by another queue initially / Stucked in Another Queue
    • Seller - Cross Question
  • Queue Routing Tracking: Record the exact Previous Queue Name, Previous Queue assigned date, and Latest assignment date to Main queue to establish operational delay timelines.

F. Need Info Abandoned Dropdowns

  • What was required?: Identify what the agent requested prior to abandonment:
    • Elaboration, Item id, Shipment ID, Tracking ID, Feed ID, Screenshot, Product Mismatch - Request UPC Documentation, Claiming Brand Ownership, Requested competitor URL, Incorrect case status update, or Other - See Summary.
  • Agent Fault: Flag Yes if associate requested redundant details or closed prematurely; flag No if the associate followed SOP.

G. BPO Coaching Dropdowns

  • Escalation Validity: Choose VALID or INVALID. If marked INVALID, you must fill out the Challenging Note detailing the SOP contradiction.
5. Audit Submission & Duration Tracking
  • Every audit records your logged-in email, audit start timestamp, submission timestamp, and total elapsed duration in minutes.
  • Submitting the audit pushes structured records to the respective AuditData_[Queue] sheet, refreshes your submitted counts, resets the form, and prepares the interface for the next audit.
6. Auditor Declaration & Formal Acceptance

By confirming below, you acknowledge that you will adhere to every rule, dropdown criterion, and sequential audit verification protocol listed above. Audits found violating these procedures are subject to calibration failure.
