QA Audit Portal

The tool my 44 QAs use every shift to fetch, audit and score support cases.

Open live demo Works in your browser with sample data. Every feature is the real code.

Problem

Audits lived in separate sheets per audit type. QAs picked cases by hand, duplicate audits happened, and scoring and root-cause entries were not consistent, which made weekly reporting slow and unreliable.

What I built

  • Serves each QA the next case assigned to them and skips cases already audited or skipped.
  • Covers 11 audit types (Reopen, FCR, CSAT, three SLA checks, General, Escalation Review, Info Request Abandoned, Resolution Delay, AHT), each with its own form and output sheet.
  • Weighted 19-question scorecard that unlocks one question at a time. If the case was not handled correctly, the audit scores 0%.
  • Linked root-cause dropdowns (type, subcategory, detail) so every audit is categorised the same way.
  • Captures audit start and end time automatically, which feeds average audit time (AAT).
  • Notice board and reminders managed from a sheet, plus a guard that trims unused cells before the workbook hits Google Sheets' 10 million cell limit.

Result

One standard audit flow for the whole team, clean data for WBR reporting, and audit time measured without manual timers.

QA Performance & Occupancy Dashboard

One screen that shows how much each QA audited, how well, how fast, and how much of their 480-minute shift was used.

Open live demo Works in your browser with sample data. Every feature is the real code.

Problem

Leads had no single view of QA productivity. Audit counts, scores, audit time and disputes sat in different sheets and were combined by hand before every review.

What I built

  • Eight live KPIs: audits, average score, average audit time, audits per day, occupancy, occupancy left, active days and valid disputes.
  • Filters for date range, fiscal week and audit type, plus multi-select filters for auditors and lines of business.
  • Valid QA disputes matched back to the original auditor by case number and shown as a dispute rate.
  • Selecting two or more QAs opens a side-by-side comparison panel.
  • Excel-style column filters and sorting, a top performer badge, and highlights for scores under 85% and audit times under 10 minutes.

Result

Weekly productivity and quality reviews run straight from the dashboard instead of manual pivot tables.

Weekly QA Performance Email

Every Monday morning, each QA gets a full report of their week, and the manager gets a Red / Yellow / Green team summary.

Open live demo Works in your browser with sample data. Every feature is the real code.

Problem

Weekly feedback to each QA was prepared by hand. It was slow, and it treated everyone the same even though QAs work different shifts, week-offs and mixes of audit types.

What I built

  • A scheduled job that pulls last fiscal week's audits, then sends every QA their report and the manager a team report in the same run.
  • Works out the client's Saturday-to-Friday fiscal week automatically.
  • Reads the shift roster, so Sunday + Monday week-offs and night shifts that cross midnight are counted on the right day and week.
  • Fair target model: full audits count against each LOB's daily target; shorter sub-audits (SLA, resolution delay, AHT) earn fixed minutes against the same 480-minute shift.
  • Work on a week-off day is shown as overtime and kept out of the top performer ranking.
  • Top performer per LOB: Green or Yellow only, at least 3 working days, ranked by target achievement, then fewer disputes.
  • A test mode that sends everything to me first before a change goes live.

Result

Weekly QA feedback went from a manual task to zero effort, with every QA measured by the same transparent rules.

QA Seating Plan

Seats two shifts of QAs across 28 desks so neighbours never audit the same line of business.

Open admin view Open QA view Works in your browser with sample data. Every feature is the real code.

Problem

Seating was planned by hand for day and night shifts. QAs from the same LOB often sat side by side, and overtime staff had no reserved space.

What I built

  • Reads the roster, keeps only the Gurugram office team, and splits QAs into day and night shifts from their shift timing.
  • Allocates 28 desks across 4 bays by interleaving lines of business, so neighbours are always from different teams.
  • Checks every desk's left and right neighbour and flags any same-LOB conflict.
  • Fixed desks for the team lead and named QAs, and a reserved overtime bay on the night shift.
  • Admin view to adjust and publish; QAs get a read-only floor map that refreshes every 30 seconds.

Result

A conflict-free floor plan for both shifts, published in one click.

Agent Feedback & Coaching Portal

Makes sure every critical or fatal audit defect gets a coaching conversation, and records who did it and when.

Open live demo Works in your browser with sample data. Every feature is the real code.

Problem

Agents with fatal (0%) audits or critical defects needed SME follow-up, but there was no record of who had been coached, and disputed audits were mixed in with real defects.

What I built

  • Access control: only listed SMEs can open the portal, and each SME sees only the agents mapped to them in the roster.
  • Two coaching queues: cases that failed any of the 6 critical parameters, and all 0% audits.
  • Pulls dispute status from the QA dispute form; valid disputes are removed and pending ones are labelled.
  • SMEs write the coaching plan in the portal; it is saved with their name and a timestamp and the case leaves the queue.
  • A leaderboard of agents with the most 0% audits and their most frequently failed parameters.

Result

A tracked coaching loop for every critical defect, and a clear list of agents who need the most support.

QA RTR Re-Audit Portal

Tests QA knowledge by having QAs re-audit cases, then measures how far each re-audit is from the original.

Open live demo Works in your browser with sample data. Every feature is the real code.

Problem

We needed a fair way to check whether QAs score the same case the same way, and to see where scoring drifts between auditors.

What I built

  • Admins assign re-audits in two modes: a QA re-checks their own past cases, or another QA's cases from the same LOB.
  • The original audit is saved as a frozen snapshot, so the re-audit is compared with exactly what was submitted.
  • Compares all 19 parameters and the root cause, and calculates score variance, parameter accuracy, and whether the case is within ±5 points.
  • Variance emails to each re-auditor, written like a business email with tables, plus a manager summary.
  • Exports the full variance report to a new Google Sheet with mismatches highlighted.

Result

Calibration became measurable: per-QA accuracy and the exact parameters where the team disagrees.

QA Re-Audit Dashboard

A second-level check on each week's audits that runs fully offline, so audit data never leaves the computer.

Open live demo Works in your browser with sample data. Every feature is the real code.

Problem

Weekly Reopen and FCR audit exports needed a quality check of the auditors themselves, and the data could not be sent to any outside service.

What I built

  • One HTML file that opens in any browser; load the week's Excel or CSV exports and everything is analysed on the machine.
  • Built-in checks plus custom rules to flag audits that need review.
  • Scoring leniency per auditor, parameter agreement, same case scored by different auditors, NA usage and audit coverage per agent.
  • Maps audit remarks to parameters, and ranks coaching priority and where agents fail most.
  • Drafts the manager and QA emails, and saves each week so trends can be compared.

Result

A repeatable weekly audit-of-the-auditors that takes minutes and keeps client data on the local machine.
